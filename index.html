How to use Microcheted

      
Pick a stitch symbol on the left, then click or drag on the grid to place it. Every cell holds one stitch. Rows read from the bottom up: row 1 runs right to left, row 2 left to right, and so on. Rounds start at the center and run counterclockwise from the marked seam.
